01

Speech recognition stays on your hardware.

A bundled Whisper model processes speech directly on your Mac. Your recording is not uploaded for transcription.

02

There is no transcript archive.

Audio and recovery text live in memory, not a notes database. Recovery text disappears when FnScribe quits.

03

No identity. No analytics.

There is no sign-in, advertising SDK, cloud speech service, or usage tracker quietly watching in the background.

04

Your clipboard comes back.

For broad compatibility, FnScribe briefly uses a concealed clipboard entry and restores your prior contents when safe.
