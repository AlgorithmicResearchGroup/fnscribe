01

Private by architecture

Your words take the shortest possible route.

From your microphone, through a model on your Mac, to the place you are typing. FnScribe has no account system, transcription server, or archive waiting in the middle.

01.1

Recognition stays on your hardware

A bundled Whisper model processes speech locally. Recordings are not sent away for transcription.

LOCAL
01.2

No transcript archive

Audio and recovery text stay in memory. There is no searchable history of what you said.

EPHEMERAL
01.3

No account or analytics

No login, usage tracker, advertising SDK, or cloud speech service is required.

QUIET
01.4

Your clipboard comes back

FnScribe briefly uses a concealed clipboard entry for broad compatibility, then restores your previous contents when safe.

RESTORED
02

One key, three moments

Dictation without breaking your train of thought.

Hold fn
  1. 01

    Place your cursor

    Start in any text field where you want the words to go.

  2. 02

    Hold and speak

    Keep holding fn, or use fn + Space for hands-free mode.

  3. 03

    Release to write

    Your speech is cleaned up locally and inserted at the cursor.

03

Small utility, useful details

Built to disappear into your workflow.

ANY/APP

Write across macOS

Use the same shortcut in native apps, browsers, terminals, and other text fields.

CLEAN/TEXT

Useful local cleanup

Spoken punctuation, lists, conservative filler removal, and explicit corrections.

YOUR/WORDS

Personal dictionary

Teach FnScribe names and exact capitalization. Entries remain in local settings.

SAFE/FALLBACK

Recovery when needed

Copy or retry the last in-memory transcript if automatic insertion misses.
